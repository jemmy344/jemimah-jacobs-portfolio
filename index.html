01 / PROFILE

Technology background.
Risk-focused thinking.

My background combines four years of front-end web development, technical delivery and quality assurance with practical GRC training. Because I've built software myself, I write security requirements that developers can implement and auditors can test.

My work focuses on identifying control gaps, structuring policies and documenting recommendations that support risk-informed decisions. This portfolio presents five training case studies and one anonymised piece of real-world data protection work. Each project is labelled by its context, so reviewers can clearly tell demonstrated skills from professional experience, and team projects show exactly which parts I wrote.
