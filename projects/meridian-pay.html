01 — The business challenge

In the training scenario, Meridian Pay processes about ₦2.5 billion a day on a 24/7 cloud platform and must settle with three partner banks by a fixed 10:00 cut-off, while its four-person security team works business hours only. A support employee clicked a phishing link, which led to suspicious account activity and payment disruption. The company had no incident response, continuity or disaster recovery plan.

02 — My individual contribution

In a five-person advisory team, I wrote the Business Impact Analysis and the Business Continuity Plan. I scored five core activities on impact, set maximum tolerable downtime (MTD), recovery time (RTO) and data loss (RPO) targets, modelled revenue at risk, mapped dependencies and recovery order, then wrote continuity arrangements and a testing programme. Teammates wrote the incident response plan, playbooks, crisis communications and DPIA.

03 — Key findings

CRITICAL
Settlement is a clock problem, not a duration problem

With a 10:00 cut-off and a 30-minute settlement run, systems must be usable by 09:00 (target) and no later than 09:30. After 09:30, technical restoration can't save that day's settlement, so contingency routes and early bank notification are the only options.

CRITICAL
Fraud monitoring must come back first

Its 10-minute tolerance is the shortest, so it is restored before payments fully reopen. If it can't be, payments run in a restricted degraded mode rather than unprotected.

CRITICAL
No 24/7 cover

Payments run around the clock but security works business hours, so every recovery target is at risk out of hours.

HIGH
Targets rely on unconfirmed capabilities

Failover, alternative settlement routes and fraud monitoring failover are unproven. The plan shows "workaround today" separately from "capability still to build".

04 — Recovery targets for the critical activities

FRAUD MONITORING MTD 10 min · RTO 5 min · RPO 0

Continuous exposure; restored first in the recovery sequence.

PAYMENT PROCESSING MTD 4 h · RTO 1 h · RPO 0

About ₦104m of transactions per hour; at 4 hours, one-sixth of daily revenue is at risk.

BANK SETTLEMENT Clock-time rule

Usable by 09:00 (target), 09:30 (latest), for the fixed 10:00 cut-off.

05 — Outcome and limits

The BIA and BCP set out proposed targets and arrangements for management approval. All figures assume evenly distributed volumes, and no failover, recovery timing or supplier capability has been tested. The documents say so explicitly rather than overstating readiness.
