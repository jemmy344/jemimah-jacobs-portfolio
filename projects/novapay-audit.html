01 — The business challenge

In the supplied NovaPay training scenario, a fintech with a customer-facing app, payment gateway, Azure services and hybrid staff needed clearer device and access governance and evidence for internal audit.

02 — My individual contribution

Within a four-person group project, I prepared the Access Control Policy in the assigned case-study role of Head of Corporate IT & Access Management. Other members worked on the BYOD Policy, gap recommendations and audit questions; these are group deliverables, not work I claim to have authored individually.

03 — Policy requirements documented

ACCESS
Least privilege and role-based access

Limit access to the minimum needed for authorised duties and require formal access requests and approvals.

REVIEW
Authentication and access review

Unique identifiers, MFA for Azure, remote, payment and privileged access, and access reviews every 6 months (90 days for privileged and third-party access).

LIFECYCLE
Joiners, movers and leavers

Address changes to permissions and timely removal of access for departing personnel.

04 — Combined group deliverables

The submission includes a BYOD Policy, my Access Control Policy, three policy gaps with suggested improvements, and five audit questions paired with the evidence an auditor would request. It shows how documented requirements can be turned into reviewable governance artefacts.

05 — Outcome and limits

The result is a collaborative training submission with a policy framework and audit prompts. It does not establish that NovaPay approved, implemented, tested or operated these controls in a live environment. Titles and approvals within the document are case-study roles.
