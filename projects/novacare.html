01 — The business challenge

In the provided training scenario, NovaCare is a growing healthcare provider operating three outpatient clinics in Ghana. It handles patient, payroll, supplier and financial information through cloud and on-premises systems. Its information security practices were described as informal, with inconsistent controls and no formal ISMS, risk register or Statement of Applicability.

02 — My work

I prepared an ISMS scope statement covering the three clinics, relevant personnel, information assets, systems and governance activities. I then documented seven gaps against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses and Annex A controls, assigned priorities, and developed recommendations to address weaknesses in policy governance, asset management, risk assessment, control selection, awareness and supplier assurance.

03 — Key findings

HIGH
Undefined ISMS scope

No formally documented boundaries for protected systems, clinics and information.

HIGH
Incomplete security policies

Policies lacked a consistent, formally approved governance foundation.

HIGH
Missing information asset inventory

Patient, payroll and supplier information was not systematically tracked or classified.

HIGH
Inconsistent risk assessment

No formal risk assessment method or central risk register was documented.

MEDIUM
No Statement of Applicability

Annex A control selections and exclusions had not been formally recorded.

MEDIUM
Irregular security awareness training

No consistent training programme for clinical, IT and administrative personnel.

MEDIUM
Inconsistent supplier assurance

Supplier security requirements were not systematically evaluated before onboarding.

04 — Recommended improvement roadmap

PLAN 0–2 months

Formalise ISMS scope, approve policy, assess risks and create asset inventory and risk register.

DO 2–5 months

Implement prioritised controls, prepare the SoA, introduce supplier checks and awareness training.

CHECK 5–7 months

Conduct an internal audit, test controls and hold management review.

ACT 7–9 months

Remediate findings, refine controls and prepare for an external Stage 1 readiness audit.

05 — Outcome and limits

The submitted capstone produced a documented scope, a seven-item prioritised gap assessment, Annex A recommendations and a phased PDCA roadmap. These are proposed improvements; the assignment does not establish that NovaCare implemented them, achieved certification or underwent a real audit.
