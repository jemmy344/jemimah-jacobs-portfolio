01 — The business challenge

The training scenario describes three risks in a regulated fintech: weak database administrator authentication, vulnerable customer mobile-app logins and a payment vendor without a formal security assessment. The company's risk appetite allows only Low risks to be accepted without treatment.

02 — My analysis

I classified the relevant assets (Restricted or Confidential), identified threats and vulnerabilities, assessed the supplied risk scenarios, recommended mitigation and identified practical controls to reduce exposure.

03 — Risk treatment recommendations

HIGH
Customer database

Require MFA for administrators, stop shared passwords and monitor unusual administrative activity.

HIGH
Mobile application

Introduce customer MFA, rate limiting, temporary account lockouts and suspicious-transaction monitoring.

HIGH
Payment vendor

Perform supplier security assessments, schedule periodic reviews and control access to shared information.

04 — Quantifying financial exposure

For the supplied database breach example, the exercise used an asset value of ₦50,000,000, a 60% exposure factor and an annualised rate of occurrence of 0.5.

₦30mSingle Loss Expectancy (SLE)
₦15mAnnualised Loss Expectancy (ALE)

Any controls costing less than about ₦15m a year that meaningfully reduce the likelihood are financially justified. These are scenario-based calculations, not observed losses or validated business forecasts.

05 — Outcome and limits

The submitted case study presents structured risk analysis, treatment recommendations and a worked SLE/ALE estimate. No live controls, remediation or financial outcomes are claimed.
